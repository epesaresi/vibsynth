Series: the component lives through all children (FDS summed, PSD averaged with the lives). Parallel: it lives through one of them (FDS and PSD enveloped). Groups can be nested.

MMVSD options

MMVSD prescribes the FDS and the kurtosis of the oscillator responses kz(fn) of the target; the Kurtosis and RMS fields are not used.

Edit options

Edit keeps the most damaging stretches of the target record (Window [s] above sets the window; default 2 s or three decay times of the slowest oscillator). Up to the compression limit the test is exact for every S-N exponent; beyond it the bands that lost damage are raised by a frequency-dependent gain.
